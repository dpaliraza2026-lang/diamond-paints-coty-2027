Section 01 • Origin & Sense of Place
Regional Botanical Anchor

A Colour Rooted in Pakistan

For 2027, Diamond Paints draws inspiration from this natural spectacle to introduce Yellow Aster, our Colour of the Year. Rooted in nature and inspired by Pakistan, Yellow Aster invites us to bring a little of that warmth and optimism into the spaces we create.

“Every year, as the warmer months arrive, the Amaltas transforms familiar landscapes with cascading clusters of golden-yellow flowers. From city streets to gardens and shaded avenues, its blossoms bring unexpected moments of beauty to everyday surroundings. A colour both vibrant and familiar, it reflects the warmth, vitality and character of the landscapes we call home.”

Avoiding Tourism Nostalgia • Architectural Daylight Utility

We deliberately avoid decorative clichés. The Amaltas establishes an authentic sense of place. In Pakistan, direct solar radiation exceeds 8 months per year; Yellow Aster (RGB: 254, 236, 123 | DCM Code: QC0083) is calibrated with soft mineral undertones to lift deep verandas and diffuse sharp noon sun without visual glare.

Section 03 • Architectural Practice • Lighting, Proportions & Placement
Design Methodology

The Art of Using Yellow

4 Architectural Studies • Uniform Grid

1. Light Bounce

Window Reveals: Deep jambs in Yellow Aster capture morning sun, bouncing indirect warmth into cool northern rooms.

Orientation: North & East

2. Overhead Glow

Ceiling & Light Wells: High yellow ceiling planes diffuse soft ambient glow downwards, softening vertical shadows.

Application: Light Wells

3. Spatial Direction

Focal Vistas: Coating terminal walls of circulation corridors in Yellow Aster anchors vision and guides flow.

Placement: Corridor Vistas

4. 60-30-10 Ratio

Proportions: 60% neutral bone plaster on major walls, 30% oak joinery/stone, and 10% Yellow Aster on a focal niche.

Discipline: 60 / 30 / 10
The 60-30-10 Surface Ratio Bar
60% Field Base (Bone White / Plaster)
30% Texture (Timber / Artista)
10% Yellow Aster
Artificial Lighting Standard
Specify luminaires between 2700K and 3000K with CRI > 92. Avoid cool lamps > 4000K to prevent colour distortion.

Section 04 • Materiality & Tactility

The Material Conversation

Positioning Rule Enforced

Correct Product Positioning: Artista — Complementing the Colour

Yellow Aster is not offered as an Artista texture. Instead, specify Yellow Aster on a smooth painted wall, paired with an Artista textured wall (e.g., Terra Tide) or architectural feature in the same room. The message is combining colour and texture to create richer interior environments.

Tactile Companions DCM & Artista Integration
1. Smooth Painted Plane: Yellow Aster in DCM Flat Matt.
2. Artista Accent: Terra Tide stucco on adjacent feature plane.
3. Architectural Timber: Smoked walnut & rift-cut white oak.
4. Natural Stone: Honed Roman travertine & grey sandstone.
